ARCHITECTURAL DETAILS

Tatami Flooring

The tatami is a traditional Japanese matting made using rice straw for the core, with a covering of woven soft rush (igusa) straw.

Tatami mats are constructed in a strict 2:1 aspect ratio, which historically governed the room sizes of traditional houses. The modular sizing creates a natural geometric harmony where rooms are referred to by the number of mats they contain. The scent of fresh igusa grass contributes to the sensory tranquility of the room.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Standard Size 910mm x 1820mm (Kanto Ratio)

MODULAR SHAKKANHO SYSTEM

Traditional planning follows the Ken (間) grid, where 1 Ken corresponds to ~1.81 meters—the length of a standard tatami mat. Rooms are assembled as multiples of these modules, fostering mathematical harmony.

ENVIRONMENT DATA
